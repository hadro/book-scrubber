Book Scrubber

Hover over a book and wiggle your mouseDrag a finger sideways across a book to flip through centuries of pages. ClickTap one to open the flipbook and make a GIF.

Works with any IIIF Presentation v2 or v3 manifest. It also understands archive.org/details/…, loc.gov/item/…, digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/…, biodiversitylibrary.org/item/…, or a bare Internet Archive identifier.

Images are loaded directly from each library's IIIF servers. Rights and attribution belong to the holding institutions; see each item's source link.

Inspired by the animated book thumbnails that used to flicker through Internet Archive search results.
